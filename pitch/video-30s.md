## The idea in one line

You say one sentence and fire comes out of a window into a real room. Then a grumpy bee whose hive you just fixed goes to the Moon anyway.

Two money shots: **embers coming out of the window into the real room** (0:13–0:19) and **"TAKE ME TO THE MOON!"** (0:21–0:27). Everything else gets us to them.

## Layouts

- **MR**: full-frame Quest capture, cropped to 9:16. What the player sees.
- **STACK**: top half is the phone over the player's shoulder (real person, real table), bottom half is the Quest capture of the same moment. This shows "it's a real table" without saying it.
- **CARD**: plain graphic, end card only.

**Who:** one player (TODO: name, someone who moves their head slowly), one person on the phone, one person on the Beat Board.

**Quest capture:**
- Built-in recording on the headset. TODO, check Sat: does passthrough show up in the recorded file, or is it black behind the virtual stuff? If it's black, try casting + screen recording on the laptop (scrcpy or Meta Quest Developer Hub) instead.
- Turn the capture resolution up as far as it goes before the hero runs. TODO: test whether the `debug.oculus.capture.*` adb properties or MQDH settings work on our headset. We're cropping 9:16 out of it, so every pixel helps.
- Move your head *slowly*. Head shake in a recording looks ten times worse than it feels.
- Good, even light on the table. Hand tracking and passthrough both get worse in dim light.

**Phone over-shoulder:**
- Vertical, 4K or 1080p, on a tripod, behind the player's right shoulder at chest height. Frame: player's hands, the table, the props.
- It records the player's voice for the what-if. Quiet room for that take.
- Clap at the start of every take, visible and audible in both recordings, to sync.

**Don't film other teams or people who didn't agree to it.** Point the phone at a wall.
