# Bee Scout habitat cue cards

Short spoken cues for the optional water-dish and nesting-block props. Keep the models as prompts for noticing habitat; they do not imply that every pollinator needs the same setup.

## Shallow water dish

**Beeswax:** “A tiny drink stop: keep the water shallow, with stones rising above it as safe landing spots. Refresh it often.”

**One-step quest:** “Can you spot a safe landing stone in the dish?”

Penn State Extension recommends a shallow dish and refreshing its water. Stones or pebbles give pollinators a place to land while drinking.

## Cavity-nesting block

**Beeswax:** “This is one kind of bee home. Many native bees nest in bare soil; some use tunnels in dead wood or hollow stems. Leave room for both.”

**One-step quest:** “Look for one patch of bare earth and one hollow stem or old piece of wood. Which habitat did you find first?”

The block represents only cavity-nesting bees. Xerces Society’s nesting guide explains that many native bees nest in the ground, while cavity nesters use dead wood, hollow stems, and similar spaces. A model of a bee hotel should never suggest replacing those natural sites.
