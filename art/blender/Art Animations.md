# Art and animations

Open Virtual Bee Swarm Art.blend for the updated models. The textures are packed in the file.

- Worker bee, bumblebee, solitary bee, and tiny wasp each have Hover, Walk, Blink, Antenna Twitch, and Shake.
- The rigged hornet and full-size wasp each have 21 clips, including flight, six-leg walk, blink, antenna twitch, abdomen wag, and thorn moves.
- The hive lid opens, the smoker lever and bellows squeeze, the spray trigger pulls in, and the rocket has an ignition and flame flicker clip.
- Flight and walking play in place. Unity handles travel, bobbing, and rocket launch movement. The pebble dish and deep bowl are static.

In Blender, select an insect rig and find its clips in the NLA Editor. Use a single track for previewing a clip on its own.

The editable Beeswax source, Unity FBX, and its full clip list are in [the Beeswax folder](beeswax/README.md).
